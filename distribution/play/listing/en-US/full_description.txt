DIW MY WALLET is an open-source wallet for digital credentials, verification, signed documents, electronic signatures and zero-knowledge proofs.

Digital credentials
- Receive credentials with OpenID4VCI 1.0 and earlier drafts: ISO mdoc, SD-JWT VC and W3C VC (including bbs-2023 selective disclosure)
- Present with OpenID4VP 1.0, ISO/IEC 18013-5 proximity (NFC, Bluetooth, QR code) and the Digital Credentials API (requests from browsers and other apps)
- Works with issuers and verifiers of the Taiwan Digital Wallet (TWDIW), Microsoft Entra Verified ID, the EUDI Wallet reference implementation, Multipaz and Bonds
- Before you share anything, see who is asking, how far they are trusted, why, how risky the request is and which fields they get
- Credential status is shown in the words of its specification

Verification
- Send online requests (OpenID4VP) or proximity requests over QR code and Bluetooth as your own verifier, with a signed verifier statement
- Read mdocs over NFC or a card reader, and check offline challenges, age proofs and zkPassport proofs

Documents and signatures
- Keep and verify PAdES, CAdES, XAdES, ASiC, OFD with electronic seals, Taiwan e-document approval files, RFC 3161 time stamps, OpenCerts, OpenPGP and C2PA content credentials of images
- Every signature shows its integrity, the trust of its certificate chain (all the way to the root), its time stamp and changes made after signing
- Sign with a Taiwan citizen digital certificate card (NFC), PIV card, OpenPGP card, PKCS#12 or a CSC remote signing service, at levels B-B to B-LTA, with a visible signature if you like
- Time stamps and revocation data are added and kept automatically; documents of the same kind are kept side by side by year
- Read e-passport chips with passive authentication
- Fetch documents from sites such as MyData and turn them into credentials you can present

Zero-knowledge proofs
- Prove you are over an age without revealing your birth date
- zkTLS website proofs, mdoc zero-knowledge presentation and field proofs of signed PDFs (zkPDF, slow on phones)
- zkPassport: verify others or answer zero-knowledge proof requests with your e-passport
- Proofs are made on your device by default, or on your own computer; circuit parameters are downloaded from the project's GitHub releases and checked against their hashes, and no code is downloaded

Privacy
- Credentials, documents and keys are stored encrypted on your device only
- Data leaves the device only when you agree to present, receive or sign, and only to the issuer, verifier or signing service you chose; viewing an OpenCerts certificate in the issuer's style sends it to the issuer's renderer
- No ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no account
- The history records field names only, never their values

Languages and platforms
- The interface is available in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese
- Desktop versions for Windows and Linux are available on GitHub
